About us

Meet the People Behind the Clean

We started Brightside with a simple belief: a clean home shouldn't cost your family's health, or your free time.

Brightside Cleaning NWA is a family-owned, locally operated company serving Bentonville, Rogers, Fayetteville, Springdale and Farmington. Behind us is a small team of vetted, background-checked cleaners who treat your home like their own.

We run Brightside on corporate-level systems, with standardized checklists, quality checks and seamless scheduling, so you get big-company reliability with the personal attention of a team that lives here too.

Our Mission

To create healthier, happier homes across NWA with honest service and non-toxic cleaning.

Our Vision

To be Northwest Arkansas's most trusted name in eco-conscious home care.

What areas of Northwest Arkansas do you serve?

We clean homes in Bentonville, Rogers, Fayetteville, Springdale and Farmington, Arkansas, plus nearby communities like Bella Vista, Centerton, Lowell and Cave Springs. Reach out if you're nearby and not sure — we'll do our best to fit you in.

What cleaning products do you use?

We primarily use Branch Basics, a plant-based, non-toxic line, and may use other fully natural products when a specific job calls for it. They're free of harsh fumes, synthetic fragrances and dyes, so they're safe for kids, pets and anyone with allergies or sensitivities.

Are you licensed, insured and background-checked?

Yes. Brightside Cleaning is fully licensed, every cleaner is background-checked and backed by workers' compensation, and our business carries comprehensive insurance coverage, so you have total peace of mind if an accident ever happens on the job. Every cleaner is carefully vetted and trusted before they ever step into your home.

Who cleans my home?

A vetted, background-checked Brightside cleaning professional who's insured, follows our detailed checklists and uses only natural, non-toxic products. We schedule the same faces whenever possible, so your cleaners get to know your home and how you like it.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on your home's size, condition, the type of clean and how often you'd like service. Recurring clients receive our best rates. Request a free estimate and we'll give you a clear, upfront price — no surprises.
