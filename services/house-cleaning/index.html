The Signature Clean

Thorough, Non-Toxic & Fully Insured

Our Standard Clean is a fresh reset: everything in our deep clean except the detail work like baseboards, blinds, cabinet faces and grout. It's designed for homes that aren't heavily built up, for clients switching from another cleaning service, and for anyone who wants to choose exactly which details matter most. Add any detail work anytime, and once it's added we'll maintain it at no additional cost.

Perfect for

  • Homes that are regularly maintained and not heavily built up

  • Anyone who wants to choose their own add-ons

How often should I schedule recurring cleaning?

Most families choose bi-weekly service, which keeps a home consistently clean at a great value. Weekly is ideal for larger households, pets or busy schedules, and monthly works well for smaller homes that just need a regular reset.

Will I have the same cleaners each time?

We schedule consistent cleaners whenever possible, so the team that comes to your home learns your preferences and priorities.

Do I need a deep clean first?

Not always. The Standard Clean works well for homes that are regularly maintained or coming from another cleaning service. If there's heavy buildup, we'll recommend starting with a Deep Clean in your estimate.

How do add-ons work?

Choose detail add-ons like baseboards, blinds or cabinet faces anytime. Once an add-on is part of your service, we maintain it on every visit at no additional cost.
