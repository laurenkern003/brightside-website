The Builder’s Finish

Thorough, Non-Toxic & Fully Insured

Northwest Arkansas is building fast, and every new home and remodel leaves behind fine drywall dust, paint specks, stickers, adhesive and debris that settles into every surface. Our post-construction and renovation cleaning is built for homeowners, builders and contractors who need a space handed over spotless, safely and on schedule, using natural products that won't leave chemical residue on brand-new finishes.

Perfect for

  • Newly built homes in Bentonville, Rogers, Springdale & beyond
  • Kitchen, bath & whole-home renovations
  • Builders, contractors & property managers
  • Homeowners moving into new construction

What's included

  • Fine construction dust removed from all surfaces, ledges & trim
  • Stickers, labels, adhesive & paint specks removed from windows and fixtures
  • Cabinets & drawers cleaned inside and out
  • Light fixtures, outlets, vents & switch plates dusted
  • Baseboards, doors & window sills wiped
  • Bathrooms & kitchens detailed, including new appliances
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped (multiple passes for dust)
  • Final walk-through ready for move-in or listing photos

Every home is different. Your estimate is tailored to your space and priorities.

When should post-construction cleaning be scheduled?

After all trades have finished their work and the space is free of heavy debris. We can coordinate with your builder or contractor on timing.

Do you work with builders and contractors?

Yes. We clean for homeowners as well as builders, contractors and property managers who need move-in-ready handovers.

Will natural products work on construction dust and residue?

Yes. Construction cleaning is mostly technique: multiple dusting passes, microfiber and careful detailing. Natural products also avoid leaving chemical residue on brand-new finishes.
