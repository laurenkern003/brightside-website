Partner With Brightside

Brightside partners with independent cleaning professionals who want the freedom of running their own business, with the clients, systems and support of an established local brand.

We partner with solo professionals and established cleaning teams who run their own businesses. Partners work with Brightside as independent contractors (1099), set their own availability, and serve our clients across Bentonville, Rogers, Fayetteville, Springdale and Farmington.

Every partner and team member is vetted and held to the Brightside Standard, so our clients get the same care in every home.

Is this an employee position?

No. We partner with independent cleaning professionals who work with Brightside as their own business owners (1099 independent contractors). You'll receive a 1099 and are responsible for your own taxes.

Where would I be working?

With homes across Northwest Arkansas, including Bentonville, Rogers, Fayetteville, Springdale, Farmington and nearby communities.

Do I need cleaning experience?

Experience is a plus, but attitude and attention to detail matter most. Tell us about yourself in the application.

What happens after I apply?

We review every application and reach out to qualified applicants to set up a conversation. Partnering with Brightside includes a background check.
