The natural difference

Why We Clean Naturally

Natural cleaning isn't a trend for us. It's how we protect the homes and families we serve across NWA.

  • Truly non-toxic products

    We primarily use Branch Basics, a plant-based, non-toxic cleaning line, so your home gets clean without a chemical cocktail left behind.

  • Safer for families & pets

    Gentle, natural formulas mean you can feel confident letting kids crawl on the floors and pets nap on the rugs as soon as we're done.

  • Cleaner air, healthier home

    No harsh sprays lingering in the air. Your home smells naturally clean, not perfumed, which makes a real difference for allergies and asthma.

  • Eco-friendly & biodegradable

    Our products are made without ammonia, chlorine bleach, synthetic fragrances or dyes. That's better for your home and for Northwest Arkansas's creeks, lakes and trails.

  • Streak-free glass & mirrors

    Natural glass cleaning leaves mirrors, windows and shower doors crystal clear without residue or artificial shine boosters.

  • Safe, natural floor care

    Non-toxic floor solutions lift dirt and grime while protecting hardwood, tile and laminate, so they're safe for little hands and paws.

  • Thoughtful dusting techniques

    Microfiber dusters and cloths trap dust instead of spreading it around, helping allergy-sensitive clients breathe easier.

  • Better for long-term home care

    Natural products avoid the residue buildup harsh chemicals leave behind, keeping your surfaces, finishes and fixtures looking newer, longer.

  • Licensed, insured & workers' comp

    We're a licensed business with comprehensive insurance, and every background-checked cleaner is backed by workers' compensation.

What cleaning products does Brightside use?

We primarily use Branch Basics, a plant-based, non-toxic cleaning line. When a specific job calls for it, we may use other fully natural products to get the best result. We never compromise on our non-toxic standard.

Are your products safe for kids, pets and allergies?

Yes. Our products are free of harsh fumes, synthetic fragrances and dyes, which makes them a great fit for homes with babies, pets, allergies, asthma or chemical sensitivities.

Do natural products really clean as well as conventional cleaners?

Absolutely. Effective cleaning comes from the right technique as much as the right product. Our vetted, trusted cleaners pair natural solutions with microfiber tools and a detailed checklist to lift dirt, grime and buildup without harsh chemicals.
